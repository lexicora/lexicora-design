## The brand is not free to use

The **name, logo (all variants), icons, tagline and store graphics** are Lexicora's identity. You may not use them as the branding of your own product, project, extension or organization, publish them to a browser extension store, or alter them in a way that suggests your work is Lexicora or is affiliated with it.

You may still:

- link to Lexicora, or mention it by name
- show the logo unmodified when writing about Lexicora, as in an article, review or screenshot
- keep unmodified copies of this repository

If you build something from the palette, theme or typography, it has to be identifiably yours: its own name, its own logo.
