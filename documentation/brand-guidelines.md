# Brand guidelines

## The brand

**Lexicora** is a browser extension for capturing, structuring and retrieving what you read on the web. Its tagline is **"Keep what you read"**.

The brand is calm and tool-like: quiet navy and white, flat shapes, and plenty of room. It is intentionally not playful or decorative.

## The logo

The mark is a square split by three diagonal cuts. It stands alone, or next to the wordmark "Lexicora" (as on the store tile in [`store/`](../store)).

Two colors only: navy `#00143d` and white.

### Variants

All files are in [`logo/`](../logo). SVG is the source; PNG is a convenience copy.

| File | Mark | Background | Use on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Lexicora_standard` | navy | white, rounded tile | Light surfaces; the default logo |
| `Lexicora_inverted` | white | navy, rounded tile | Dark surfaces |
| `Lexicora_standard_square`, `_square_v2` | navy | white, square tile | Where the platform applies its own rounding |
| `Lexicora_inverted_square` | white | navy, square tile | The same, on dark |
| `Lexicora_standard_no-bg` | white | none | Dark backgrounds |
| `Lexicora_inverted_no-bg` | navy | none | Light backgrounds |
| `Lexicora_alt1` | navy | none, rounded bounds | Light backgrounds |
| `Lexicora_alt2` | white | none, rounded bounds | Dark backgrounds |
| `Lexicora_alt3`, `alt4` | navy, white | none, square bounds | Light, dark backgrounds |

Note that `standard` and `inverted` name the tile, not the mark: a `no-bg` file named "standard" is the white mark, for a dark surface.

### Other assets

- [`icons/`](../icons): the extension's toolbar icons at 16, 32, 48, 96 and 128 px. `icons/dark/` has the variants for dark browser toolbars.
- [`store/`](../store): the graphics made for the browser extension stores (512 px icons and the small promotional tile).

### Do not

- recolor the logo, add effects, shadows or gradients
- stretch, rotate, outline or redraw it
- place it on a busy or low-contrast background
- use it as the logo of your own product, or in a way that suggests affiliation with Lexicora
